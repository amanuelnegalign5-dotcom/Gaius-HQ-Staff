# Administrator governance

Country Director and International Director are full administrators. Full administrators can edit non-source reports, manage users, attendance, announcements, projects, document metadata, and Director comments.

Full administrators can delegate selected permissions to limited administrators: Reports, Director Comments, Attendance Management, Announcements, Projects, Documents, and User Administration.

Full administrator rights may be transferred to a successor through the Admin Control page. The transfer records the granting administrator and time. For production, enforce this operation with re-authentication, multi-factor authentication, server-side authorization, and an immutable audit trail.

The six preserved October source reports cannot be overwritten, even by full administrators; administrative comments can be added instead.
